# Covert Crew in Tab: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

Covert Crew in Tab is a browser extension that adds a small family of animated characters to the web pages you visit. This policy explains what the extension looks at and what it does with it.

## The short version

**Covert Crew in Tab does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.** Everything it does happens on your own computer. It makes no network requests and has no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.

## What the extension looks at, and why

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **The layout of the page you're on** (where images, headings and buttons are) | So the characters can walk, climb and stand on things | Used in the moment, never saved |
| **Search terms** in the page address on search sites (for example `?q=pizza`), or in a search box when you press Enter | So the characters can react to the topic (for example, getting hungry when you search for pizza) | Matched against a built-in word list in the moment, never saved or sent anywhere |
| **How many keys you press** (a count only) | So the characters can cheer you on while you're typing | The extension never reads *which* keys you press or what you type |
| **Mouse position** | So the characters can look at your cursor, be picked up and be petted | Used in the moment, never saved |

## What the extension saves

The extension saves your settings in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your computer:

- Which characters are switched on, their names and their size
- Whether search reactions, typing cheers and climbing are turned on
- Websites where you've chosen to hide the characters
- Any pictures you upload to change how a character looks

This data never leaves your computer. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

## Permissions

- **Access to the sites you visit:** needed so the characters can appear on every page.
- **Storage:** saves your settings, as described above.
- **Active tab:** lets the extension's popup see which site you're on, so you can hide the characters there, and send commands like "Gather" or "Party" to that tab.

## Children

The extension doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
